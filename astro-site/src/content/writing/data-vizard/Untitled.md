We hosted a third in-person workshop this year at VizChitra 2026. This time it was a bit different. Usually the workshop is structured around getting folks to create their first online artifact using AI. But for VizChitra, we decided to get people to visualize a dataset into an interactive artifact. This meant we had to teach people the process behind making a visualization before asking them to actually create one.

That meant introducing the process behind making a good visualization before asking them to actually create one.
